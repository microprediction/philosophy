## "Only language" (issue #33; credit Stuart Reynolds)

Done 2026-10-09 as docs/only-language.html, written from the essay alone. The phrase "only language" is not attributed to Reynolds on the page, because his LinkedIn reply was never read. Defendants are Browning and LeCun (Noema 2022) and LeCun on Fridman #416; quotes were checked against the fetched pages and arXiv abstracts.

- Source of the suggestion: Stuart Reynolds's reply in the thread under Piotr Migdał's LinkedIn post "Pure mathematics, in my view, is not science, not craft - it is art" (8 Oct 2026), https://lnkd.in/p/gUMc45Jc. **His reply is not publicly readable** (LinkedIn shows only top-level comments without a login), so his wording is still to be copied in. Do not paraphrase him from memory.
- His essay: Stuart Reynolds, "AI Can't? In Defense of Behaviorism", Medium, 29 June 2024, https://medium.com/@stuart-reynolds/in-defense-of-behaviorism-44fdbd7a4087. Medium blocks scripted fetches; the quotes below are from the live text Peter pasted on 2026-10-09. Quote briefly and link; do not reproduce the essay.
  - "Stu's Golden Rule for AI Tests", current wording: "If it's not a standard for human intelligence when your family can't do it, it's not a standard for intelligence when AI can't do it." (An archived 2024 copy has "human intelligence" in both clauses; use the current wording.)
  - The sensory version of the rule, which is the rebuttal to "only language": "AI so stoopid. AI can't hear." / "Your granddaddy is deaf. Are you calling him deficient in intelligence? Is that what intelligence is?" and "AI so stoopid. AI can't see." / "Your nana is practically blind. Are you calling her stupid too?"
  - "Prove that you are not simply a meat chauvinist — you must be happy to run the test against your family at your next family dinner."
  - On method: "If you want to make claims that some system fails to meet some level of intelligence, particularly human intelligence, do as a behaviorist does — simply produce that test and run it."
  - The essay does not use the phrase "only language". That wording is from his LinkedIn reply, still to be copied in.
- Argument for the page: lacking a sense is not lacking a mind. If learning without sight or hearing disqualified understanding, it would disqualify people; pair Reynolds's rule with Kim, Elli and Bedny (PNAS 2019), already cited on the stochastic-parrot page.
- Overlap to manage: the stochastic-parrot page already rebuts "trained on form alone" (Bender and Koller), and next-token rebuts the capability claim. An "only language" page should take the neighbouring claim that language itself is too thin a medium for thought (for example Fedorenko, Piantadosi and Gibson, "Language is primarily a tool for communication rather than thought", Nature 2024; LeCun on language as a low-bandwidth channel). ## Chinese Room: lines of attack not yet on the page

- The brain is already in a Chinese room (the skull): it only ever receives spike trains. Kills Searle's answer to the robot reply.
- Leibniz's Mill (1714) is the same argument; Leibniz accepted that it refutes materialism, Searle wants it both ways.
- The simulated Searle writes the same paper, for the same causal reasons, so his first-person conviction is not evidence.
- Neuron replacement (Pylyshyn 1980; Searle's 1992 answer requires the mind to be causally cut off from its own reports).
- Program vs process: "syntax has no causal powers" is true of the listing, not the running process. Chalmers on implementation vs the "wall implements WordStar" claim.
- Evolution can only select on behaviour; a behaviourally inert "real understanding" could never have been selected for.
- No state, no learning: a real speaker remembers and learns mid-conversation, so the "book" must be a huge mutable state.
- Searle concedes machines can think; the remaining target (program independent of any implementation) is held by nobody.
- Split-brain patients: two understanders in one skull is a clinical finding, not an ad hoc move.

## "Every example" (from Seth 2026)

- Seth, "Conscious artificial intelligence and biological naturalism", BBS 49 e315 (2026). PDF in Peter's Downloads. p. 15: "Simply noting that all known examples of conscious systems are biological and alive suggests that biological naturalism is no more speculative than computational functionalism." The sample is one evolutionary lineage on one planet; every known conscious system is also carbon-based, and Seth rejects carbon chauvinism (4.5).
- Same page: "Our psychological biases are more likely to lead to false positives than false negatives", directly after the record of withholding moral status from animals and "sometimes even from other humans", which is a record of false negatives.
